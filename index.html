I'm Nishit Kumar, a final-year B.Tech student at the National Institute of Technology Karnataka (NITK), Surathkal, with a strong focus on Data Science, Machine Learning and Generative AI.

My work centers on Generative AI and Agentic AI systems — I design and build multi-agent pipelines that turn complex reasoning into reliable, production-ready applications.

My foundation spans Python, SQL and core ML/DL frameworks (TensorFlow, Keras, Scikit-learn), extending into the GenAI stack — Hugging Face Transformers, FAISS, ChromaDB, LangChain/LangGraph and the Groq API — alongside a solid grounding in statistical modeling, hypothesis testing and feature engineering.

Beyond code, I've led marketing outreach for Incident, NITK's annual cultural fest, and built & maintained the platform for the Hindi Evam Sanskrit Club. I placed in the top 3% of JEE Advanced/Mains among over a million candidates and have solved 400+ problems across LeetCode and GeeksforGeeks.
